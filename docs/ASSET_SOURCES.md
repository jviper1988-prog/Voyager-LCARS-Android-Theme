No distressed texture is claimed as an exact match. Body/app names use Android's built-in sans-serif. The brewery name and label artwork are not included in this launcher.

## Fortress wallpaper
AI-created via built-in image generation; no model name reported. Source session artifact: exec-816a45e3-e344-406f-9721-a849b5f032e8.png. Local packaged asset: app/src/main/res/drawable-nodpi/fortress.webp. Generated earlier in the design exploration; retained as a prototype asset. Its prompt used a franchise-adjacent mood reference, so review/replace it before commercial release if necessary. Do not claim legal clearance.

Exact generation prompt:
> Create a production Android phone wallpaper, portrait 1440x3120 composition, no text, no UI, no icons, no clock, no phone frame, no borders. Daedric-inspired dark fantasy environment: towering jagged black volcanic fortress centered in lower middle distance, weathered obsidian cliffs and scorched path, thin glowing ember-red fissures, dramatic dark red eclipse behind the tallest spire in the upper third, charcoal storm clouds. Sophisticated near-black palette with restrained crimson light; highly detailed cinematic painted realism. Upper 15% and lower 35% should have darker quieter regions that allow real launcher clock and app icons to be overlaid legibly. Match a sinister blackened-metal and red-glow phone theme, not a sci-fi console. One seamless wallpaper asset only.

## Crest and icons
The small angular gate/ember crest is newly drawn as an Android vector for this prototype. App tiles read icons from the user's installed applications at runtime; no third-party app icons are bundled. Their owners retain rights. No copyrighted franchise audio is included.

Wallpaper is encoded as WebP for APK size; no change to the composition.
